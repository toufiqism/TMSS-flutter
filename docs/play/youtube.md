## Settings that matter

- **Visibility: Public.** Play cannot embed an unlisted or private video, and the listing
  silently drops the promo if the URL is not publicly playable.
- **Made for kids: No.** It is a workplace app.
- **Category:** Science & Technology, or Howto & Style — either is accepted.
- Paste the watch URL (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…`) into Play Console →
  Main store listing → Promo video. A `youtu.be` short link also works; a Shorts URL
  does not.
